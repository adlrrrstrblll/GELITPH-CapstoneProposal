Photographs are used under the Unsplash License: https://unsplash.com/license. Downloaded versions are resized and compressed; CSS crops them and displays muted color. Attribution is also available inside the exhibit through **Image & media credits**.

`remembering.webm` is an eight-second silent photographic study created for this prototype from Sadia Afreen's credited photograph. Its on-screen text and exhibit caption mark it as SAMPLE illustrative media. It is not footage submitted by a migrant or family.

`sunday-narration.wav` is an offline computer-voiced reading of an original fictional excerpt from **The space between Sunday calls**. Windows speech synthesis produced the included voice; it is explicitly labeled as a computer-voiced SAMPLE. It is not a recording of a real contributor.

The six SAMPLE narratives and associated pseudonyms were created for this prototype. They are fictional, are not interviews, and are separate from the collection target of at least 10 genuine personal accounts.

The box favicon is an original, code-drawn geometric mark. Fonts use Georgia and the visitor's system sans-serif; no remote font service is needed.
