# Content source register

Researched and accessed 16 November 2026 (Asia/Manila).

The ten built-in entries are short third-person editorial summaries of the linked publications. Entry titles and Filipino versions were prepared for this project. They are not verbatim passages, new interviews, contributor submissions, or statements written by the people named in the entries. The “Account of” label identifies the person described, rather than assigning authorship of our summary to them.

Pairs are thematic comparisons of independent accounts, not relatives or participants in a shared conversation. The “stayed” classification describes the period covered by the account, even when the writer later traveled abroad. Stable sample IDs are retained only for existing routes and local likes.

## Selection notes

- **What arrives inside a balikbayan box:** VERA Files republishes an essay originally written for PCIJ in 2005.
- **Waiting for the next homecoming:** Uses the Rodrigo and Ma. Sheena Blanca section, not the separate Consignado family interview.
- **Starting again after returning home:** Uses Elsilyn Nitura’s account; the article also profiles Aloha Gozon.
- **A warm hand in an Ontario winter:** originally published 2022-02-24; consulted version updated 2026-09-22.
- **Growing up with a father overseas:** The stayed perspective concerns her childhood while her father worked overseas; her later exchange semester is contextualized separately.
